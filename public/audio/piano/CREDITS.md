Downloaded from https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/ on 2026-10-17.
See SOURCE_README.txt for the original project information.

Zengo redistributes 30 MP3 samples supplied by Tone.js, without further
audio edits. This is a single sampled dynamic layer, spaced in minor thirds
across A0–C8; intervening notes are repitched by Tone.Sampler. Velocity controls
playback level. This compact instrument does not include the full Salamander
library's velocity layers, pedal noises or sympathetic resonance samples.
